Employee Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 20 days of paid leave per year.

Employees must submit a leave request at least 3 working days before the planned leave date.

Managers are responsible for approving or rejecting leave requests.

Emergency leave may be requested without the usual 3-day notice requirement.


Employee Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.

Remote work must be approved by the employee's manager.

Employees working remotely must remain available during normal working hours.

Certain roles may not be eligible for remote work because of operational requirements.


Employee Working Hours Policy

The standard working schedule is 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.

Employees are expected to complete their assigned working hours.

Flexible working hours may be available with manager approval.


Employee Benefits Policy

Employees are eligible for health insurance benefits after completing the required eligibility period.

The company provides basic health coverage to eligible employees.

Employees should contact Human Resources for questions about benefits.


Employee Expense Policy

Business-related expenses may be reimbursed when they are properly documented.

Employees must submit expense claims with receipts.

Expense claims should be submitted within the required reimbursement period.
